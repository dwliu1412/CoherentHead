CoherentHead: Text-to-Animatable Gaussian Heads via Surface-Consistent Diffusion Guidance

Paper Code coming soon! arXiv

Abstract

Driven by rapid advancements in 2D-lifting methods, text-guided generation of high-quality, animatable 3D head avatars has emerged as a crucial frontier for immersive applications in gaming, film, and social media. However, existing methods struggle to synthesize fine-grained appearance details and achieve accurate driving due to ambiguities in 2D diffusion predictions during 3D avatar distillation, leading to unrealistic and unnatural animations. We present CoherentHead, a coarse-to-fine framework for text-driven animatable Gaussian heads. At the coarse stage, we introduce Surface-Consistent Score Distillation (SCSD), which samples shared noise on the canonical FLAME surface and reuses its components across corresponding views and expressions. This ties the injected noise to the facial surface rather than to image coordinates. Finally, UV-guided Cross-Pose Refinement (UV-CPR) transports appearance across head poses, uses the resulting consistency energy to correct the last diffusion denoising step, and distills view-consistent targets back into the avatar. Experiments show that CoherentHead improves on existing methods in generating animatable 3D head avatars from text.

Method

Overview of CoherentHead, a coarse-to-fine framework for generating animatable Gaussian heads from text. (a) Starting from a text-generated portrait, we reconstruct a static head and fit a FLAME-bound Gaussian avatar. The FLAME-UVD representation parameterizes Gaussian positions with learnable surface positions and normal offsets, supporting facial animation and canonical surface correspondence. (b) Surface-Consistent Score Distillation (SCSD) shares noise samples across corresponding surface regions in different views, poses, and expressions. The resulting diffusion guidance helps recover details in initially occluded regions while encouraging consistent appearance. (c) UV-guided Cross-Pose Refinement (UV-CPR) further enhances texture detail by aligning images through canonical UV correspondences and measuring appearance differences in jointly visible regions. The consistency gradient adjusts the latent entering the final denoising step while the diffusion model remains frozen. Finally, the refined multi-view images are leveraged to optimize the current 3DGS.
